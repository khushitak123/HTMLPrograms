Merry Christmas

Merry Christmas all of you!

Lights that sparkle, stars that gleam,
Christmas feels just like a dream.
Warm sweet laughter fills the air,
Love and joy are everywhere.

Gifts are wrapped with tender care,
Happiness is meant to share.
May your days be calm and bright—
Merry Christmas, full of light.


Christmas comes with gentle cheer,
A joyful time that ends the year.
Homes glow bright with red and green,
A peaceful, happy winter scene.

We gather close with those we love,
Thankful for blessings from above.
Songs of hope fill every night,
Candles shine with soft warm light.

It’s more than gifts beneath the tree—
Christmas is love, and harmony.

Christmas is a joyful festival celebrated on December 25th to mark the birth of Jesus Christ. It is a time when people decorate their homes with lights, stars, and Christmas trees. Families come together to share food, exchange gifts, and enjoy happy moments. Many people sing carols, visit churches, and spread kindness during this season. Christmas teaches us the values of love, peace, and giving. It is a festival that brings warmth and happiness to everyone, even in the cold winter season.
